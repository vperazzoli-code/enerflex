Executive Summary

Enerflex Ltd. is a Calgary-based energy infrastructure company with significant opportunities for lean consulting services. The company operates in gas processing, compression, and power generation across North America and internationally, with $2.41 billion in revenue (2024) but faces operational challenges that lean methodologies could address.

Company Profile

Calgary-headquartered with major operations in Houston, TX, serving global energy markets with gas processing and compression solutions.

Market Position

$1.3B backlog with 20-25% EBITDA margins, trailing competitors achieving 40-60% margins.

Lean Opportunity

Supply chain delays, project overruns, and operational inefficiencies create compelling transformation case.

Key Operational Challenges

Supply Chain Disruptions

  • Multiple quarterly reports cite "supply chain interruptions leading to delays in receiving materials and parts"
  • Tariff impacts affecting production timelines
  • Working capital cycle at 105 days indicating inefficient inventory management
  • Large working capital fluctuations impacting cash flow
